About TrueNorth Guide

A decade of checking in, so your trip starts on the right foot

TrueNorth Guide launched in 2015 from a kitchen table in Calgary, with a simple idea: cut the marketing gloss and tell travellers what a hotel is genuinely like to stay in. We're not a booking engine, and no hotel chain owns a share of us.

Our small team of correspondents pays their own way, keeps a low profile at check-in, and writes about what actually happens between the front door and the checkout desk — the brilliant, the frustrating and the surprising. We never publish prices, we never take money for kind words, and every review carries the name of the writer who slept there.

When a link on our site sends you off to book a room, we may earn a small commission from the hotel. That's what keeps the guide free to read — and it has never once bought a better score.

Our method

How every TrueNorth review gets made

The same four steps sit behind every rating you'll read here.

01

We book & pay

Rooms are reserved exactly like any guest's — no comps, no "media rate", no heads-up to the hotel that a reviewer is on the way.

02

We stay the night

Our writer lives in the room for at least a night, testing the bed, the shower, the walls, the Wi-Fi and how service holds up after dark.

03

We weigh the crowd

We read hundreds of recent guest reviews so a single great — or grim — night never skews the verdict.

04

We say it plainly

A signed, first-person review with a clear verdict: praise where it's earned, and honesty where it isn't.
